Kith Connect privacy policy

Draft: the person running this helper should review it, add their contact details, and keep it up to date.

What this service is

Kith Connect helps a family's self-hosted Kith (an AI companion that runs on the family's own home server) connect to their child's own Google account. It is a pass-through: it holds the Google app's client credentials and nothing else.

Google user data

  • Collected and stored by this service: none. It has no database and keeps no tokens, codes or account details. Sign-in codes and tokens pass through it only to be handed to the family's own Kith, and are not logged.
  • Accessed by the family's Kith (on their own server, not by this service), only with the scopes the family's grown-up switches on: files Kith creates or the child chooses with the Google Picker (drive.file); events in the child's primary calendar (calendar.events); and task lists (tasks). Gmail, contacts, photos and Classroom are never requested.
  • How it's used: only to provide Kith's features to that family: saving what the companion makes, editing files the child picked after they confirm each change, adding calendar events and homework tasks they confirm. Never for advertising, never sold, never shared, never used to train AI models.
  • Google user data handled by Kith follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Either the child or their grown-up can disconnect Google in Kith at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in Google Drive stay theirs.
