Kith Connect

This is the sign-in helper that lets a family's own Kith connect to their child's Google account, without each family setting up Google themselves.

What it does

  • Passes the Google sign-in back to the family's own Kith, which runs on their own home server.
  • About once an hour, gets that Kith a fresh access pass from Google.

What it never does

  • It stores nothing: no accounts, no tokens, no logs of them. It has no database.
  • It never sees anyone's files, chats, calendar or homework. Each family's Kith talks to Google directly.
  • It can only ask Google for: the files Kith makes or the child picks (drive.file), and, if the family's grown-up switches them on, the child's own calendar events and task lists.

Families who'd rather not use this helper can make their own Google client instead (see Kith's docs/google.md).
