Terms of use

Last updated: 25 November 2026

These terms are for everyone who uses Kith: young people and the grown-ups who look after them. We've tried to write them so you can both read them together. If anything's unclear, ask your grown-up, or ask us on GitHub.

If you run your own Kith

Kith is software that families install on their own computers. Whoever installs and runs a copy of Kith (a person or an organisation) is its operator, and is solely responsible for:

  • their server, its security, its updates and its backups;
  • their Claude API account with Anthropic: how it's used, what it costs, and following Anthropic's terms;
  • choosing and supervising the young people who use it, getting consent from their parents or carers, and safeguarding them;
  • following data protection law as the data controller (UK GDPR, or the law where they live), including writing their own privacy notice;
  • their settings, the connections they switch on, and any changes they make to the code.

Kith's authors and contributors provide the software as it is, under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with no warranty. They don't run anyone else's Kith, can't see anyone else's data, and accept no liability for how another copy is run, as far as the law allows.

What Kith is

Kith is an AI companion: a computer program that chats, helps with homework, makes things and checks in kindly. It's open source, which means anyone can read the code, and it's self-hosted, which means it runs on a family's own computer at home rather than on a big company's servers. The thinking is done by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which Kith uses through Anthropic's API.

Who it's for

Kith is made for young people, with a parent or carer who runs it for them. The grown-up sets it up, chooses the limits, and agrees to these terms on the young person's behalf. If you're the young person: you don't need to sign anything, but please read the next few sections, because they're about you.

Using it kindly

Please don't use Kith to:

  • hurt, bully, threaten or trick anyone, including yourself;
  • make anything that's sexual, hateful or illegal;
  • try to get around its safety features, the safety check or the grown-up's settings (for example by telling it to "pretend" the rules don't apply);
  • pass off its work as your own where that isn't allowed, such as coursework (it will help you learn, but it won't do coursework for you).

If something goes wrong, or you're worried about anything, tap Grown-up?. It's on every screen.

It's an AI, and it can be wrong

Kith always says it's an AI, not a person. AI can sound sure of itself and still be wrong, so check anything important with a teacher, a grown-up or a trusted source. Kith isn't medical, legal or emergency advice. In an emergency, call 999. If you need someone to talk to, Childline is free on 0800 1111.

Keeping you safe

Every message is checked by a separate safety check, both ways. Everyday chats stay private, but if something suggests you might be in danger or not okay, Kith lets your grown-up know. It's honest about this: you're told first, in that same reply, with the exact words your grown-up will see and why. There are no secret reports, and you can see every report in "What … can see". You won't be in trouble.

What the grown-up is responsible for

If you run Kith for your family, you're responsible for:

  • the server it runs on, keeping it updated, and keeping backups private;
  • your own Claude API key, the spending limits you set in the Anthropic Console and in Kith, and following Anthropic's terms (which need an organisation under its Commercial Terms for use by under-18s);
  • the settings you choose: quiet hours, helplines, connections and the safeguards document;
  • following the law where you live, including data protection law, and telling your child how Kith works.

Open source, and no warranty

Kith is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). You can use, study, change and share it under that licence. If you run a changed version for other people over a network, the licence asks you to offer them its source code.

Kith is provided as it is, without any warranty, to the extent the law allows. Its safety features are careful, but no filter catches everything, which is why a grown-up is always in the loop.

Not legal advice: check with a professional if you run Kith for others.

Changes, and stopping

Kith is early software and it will change: features may be added, changed or removed. Your grown-up can switch it off or delete it at any time. If these terms change, the date at the top will change too, and important changes will be explained in the release notes on GitHub.

Not Anthropic

Kith uses Claude through Anthropic's API, but it isn't made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Anthropic. "Claude" is Anthropic's name for its AI models.

Questions and problems

For questions about Kith, open an issue on Kith's GitHub repository. Please don't put anything personal in an issue: they're public. To report a security or child-safety problem privately, follow SECURITY.md.

See also the privacy policy.
