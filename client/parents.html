A tour of Kith Parent

Kith Parent is the grown-up's side of Kith: everything you need to keep your child safe and the spending sensible, and none of the snooping. Here's every part of it, with real screenshots.

spending, set-up and reports at a glance

Overview

The first thing you see: this month's spending against the hard stop, with a projection for the rest of the month, and a set-up checklist that ticks off thinking, reports and notifications.

Below it are this week's chat, desk and Low power figures, the latest reports, and a private test chat to check the companion is really working.

three levels, and your child is always told first

Reports

Reports come at three levels: Right now for possible danger (straight to your phone, even in quiet hours), Today for things like bullying or not eating, and Just so you know for a gentle heads-up.

Before any report is sent, your child is told, in that same reply, exactly what you'll see and why. They see the same list you do.

what the week was about, not what was said

Weekly summary

Every Sunday evening, a few lines at topic level: what they revised, what they made, how they seemed. Never quotes from their chats.

Your child sees exactly the same summary, so there's nothing said about them behind their back.

a weekly allowance, low power, and a hard stop

Budgets and energy

You set a weekly allowance, split between chat and the companion's desk, and a hard stop for the month, a little under the limit on your Anthropic account.

When Full power runs low, Kith switches smoothly to Low power (a smaller model) instead of stopping. Your child sees energy, never money.

private on the lock screen, unless you choose

Notifications on your phone and Mac

Choose which report levels reach you, plus the weekly summary and budget alerts. Right now reports always come through; everything else waits until quiet hours end.

By default the lock screen only says something needs you, never what. You can choose to show the one-line summary instead.

rules that sit under the personality

Safeguards

The safeguards document comes first in every conversation, ahead of everything your child writes and every skill. You can read all of it, edit it, or go back to the built-in version.

Add your own family rules too, like “no horror stories after 8pm”. Whatever it says, the separate safety check, reports and Grown-up? keep working.

teach it how your family does things

Skills

Skills are short how-tos you write: how to help with maths step by step, how to run a quick quiz, how to wind down at bedtime. Three examples are ready to start from.

Give a skill a command and your child can type it, like /quiz. Your safeguards always come first.

look only, or allowed to act

Connectors

Connect the companion to things at home: the bedroom lights through Home Assistant, the family calendar in Kin, Jellyfin, MCP servers, a calculator and a dictionary. Only you can add or switch them on.

Each is look only or can act. Nothing that spends money, messages people or posts online is ever offered, and every use is logged.

a block list, a live view, and every page visited

Pip's browser

The companion can look things up for tasks your child asks for, in a real browser on its desk that never signs in, types or downloads by itself. You choose what's blocked, or keep it to a safe list of sites.

Watch it live as it reads, and see every page it opened, when, and what it was for.

approve new ones, and see progress

Languages

When your child asks to learn a new language, nothing is practised until you say yes. You can also add or remove languages yourself and set their level.

You see words learned, days practised and the topics, the same summary your child sees. The practice chats themselves stay private.

every extra is a separate switch

Google

If your child connects their own Google account, Kith saves what it makes to a Kith folder in their Drive. Editing Docs, their Calendar and a Homework list in Google Tasks are each off until you switch them on.

Every change needs your child's yes first, and nothing is ever deleted, shared or emailed.

sign out one person, or everyone

Who's signed in

See everyone with Kith open, and on how many devices. If a phone is lost or a computer is shared, sign that person out, or sign out everywhere except the device you're on.

Grown-up sessions last 7 days, so the parent view never stays open for long.

its own window, and notifications even when it's closed

The Kith Parent Mac app

Kith Parent is also a Mac app, with its own window and Dock icon. Close the window and it keeps running in the menu bar, so reports still arrive as Mac notifications.

On a phone, add Kith to the Home Screen for the same view. Download the Mac apps.

on purpose

What you never see

  • Their everyday chats. Homework, stories, the things they're thinking about: those stay between them and the companion. You get reports when something matters, and the weekly summary.
  • Their memories. What the companion remembers is theirs to read, add to and delete.
  • Their language practice chats. You see progress, not the conversations.

Your child can see exactly what you see, any time, in “What Mum can see”. There are no secret reports.
