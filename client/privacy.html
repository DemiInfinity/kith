what's kept, and who sees it

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This is how the Kith at this address looks after information about the young person who uses it and their grown-up. The short version: it lives at home, everyday chats stay private, nothing is sold, and nothing is used to train AI.

Every Kith is run by the family it belongs to, on their own home server. This policy describes how Kith works when a family runs it. Whoever runs a Kith is responsible for it and for the information on it: if you run Kith yourself, you're the data controller and need your own privacy notice (you can adapt this one).

Who's responsible

Each family's own Kith is run by that family, on their home server. They are the data controller for it: they decide what's kept and for how long, within the limits below. Kith's authors don't run other families' servers and can't see anything on them.

What's kept

  • Who you are when you sign in: your first name, an account ID and the email address from the family's sign-in service (Pocket ID, say), so Kith knows whether you're the young person or the grown-up. They're stored with your session, and deleted when you sign out or the session ends (after 30 days for the young person, 7 for the grown-up).
  • Chats: kept for 30 days (the grown-up can make it shorter, down to 1 day), then deleted. Check-ins and the log of tools used are kept the same way.
  • Memories: the things the companion remembers about you. They're yours: you can see them, add to them and delete them, and your grown-up can't read them.
  • Reports, weekly summaries and the companion's journal: kept for up to a year, so you can both look back.
  • Spending records: what each request to Claude cost, so the budget works. Kept for 400 days, so a year's spending can be compared, then deleted.
  • Desk files: the things the companion makes on its desk, and its browser's history and saved site data (the grown-up can clear these).
  • Google, only if connected: a Kith folder in your own Google Drive, and, if your grown-up switches them on, your Calendar and a Homework list in Google Tasks. Kith only uses files you pick or that it made.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a push subscription for your device, so check-ins and reminders can reach it.

Voice is turned into text and back again on the home server, in memory. Recordings are never stored. Personal details such as addresses, phone numbers, emails and school names are removed from messages before they're stored or sent anywhere, and the companion never asks for them.

Who it's shared with

  • Anthropic, to think: messages go to the Claude API under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, which means they aren't used to train AI. Anthropic may keep inputs and outputs for a short time for safety and abuse monitoring under its own policies (see anthropic.com/legal).
  • Google, only if you connect your Google account, and only for the things described above.
  • Push services (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your device), only to deliver notifications. They carry a short message, never your chats.

That's all. There are no adverts, nothing is sold, and nobody builds a profile of you. Kith loads no fonts, trackers or adverts from anywhere else (the only outside script is Google's file picker, and only if Google is connected).

What the grown-up can and can't see

Can see

  • Reports when something matters, with the exact words you were told first
  • A weekly summary at topic level, the same one you see
  • Spending, in money (you see energy)
  • Everything on the desk, the journal, the browser live and the pages it opened
  • Every tool or connection the companion used
  • Language progress: levels, words learned and topics

Can't see

  • Your everyday chats
  • Your memories
  • Your practice chats in other languages

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Age Appropriate Design Code, young people have the same rights over their information as adults, explained in a way they can understand. You can:

  • see what's kept about you (your memories and "What … can see" show most of it);
  • ask for something to be corrected or deleted (you can delete memories yourself);
  • ask questions about how your information is used.

The quickest way is to ask your grown-up, who runs this Kith. For questions about how Kith itself works, open an issue on GitHub (without anything personal in it). You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office if you're unhappy with how your information is handled.

Cookies

Kith uses two cookies, both needed for it to work, so there's nothing to agree to: kith_sid keeps you signed in, and kith_confirm lasts a few minutes while an installed app finishes signing in (it's only for the "Finish signing in?" page, and is gone once you answer). There are no tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics.

Keeping it secure

Only the family's own sign-in gets in. Saved keys, such as the Claude API key and Google tokens, are encrypted on the server. The companion's desk runs in a separate box with no direct internet. Backups aren't made automatically: whoever runs this Kith makes them by hand, and is responsible for keeping them private, away from the server, and for deleting old ones (a backup keeps chats past the 30 days until it's deleted).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and anything important will be explained in plain words, to you and your grown-up, before it applies. See also the terms of use.
